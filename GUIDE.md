# GTA VI Trophy Hunting Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to the ultimate guide for conquering the sun-drenched streets of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI. Achieving the Platinum trophy in this sprawling masterpiece requires a blend of criminal ambition, tactical precision, and a lot of patience. This manual is designed to steer you through the complexities of the game's reward system without stripping away the joy of discovery. Whether you are a series veteran or a newcomer to the Vice City lifestyle, these pages contain everything you need to know. We will cover every aspect from the main narrative arc to the most obscure side activities. Prepare yourself for a long but rewarding journey toward becoming a true legend of the underworld.

## Getting Started
Before you dive headfirst into the chaos, it is essential to understand the basic mechanics that drive progression. Start by familiarizing yourself with the new partner system, as many trophies are tied to your synergy with your companion. It is highly recommended to keep multiple save files, especially before embarking on major heist sequences or making pivotal story choices. The game world is massive, so investing in fast vehicles early on will save you significant travel time between missions. Don't feel pressured to complete every side activity immediately; most can be tackled at your own pace during the endgame. Keep a steady eye on your in-game statistics menu to track your progress toward 100% completion. Lastly, always carry enough ammunition and body armor to handle the unpredictable escalations of Leonida’s law enforcement.

## Story Trophies
The narrative backbone of GTA VI is divided into several chapters, each offering its own set of unique trophies. Most of these are unmissable and will unlock naturally as you progress through the main missions of Jason and Lucia’s journey. Focus on finishing the "Gone Fishing" prologue to establish your footing in the world before moving into the heavier heists. Missions like "The Cartel Heist" and "Breakfast Run" are key milestones that signify your rising influence in the criminal hierarchy. While it might be tempting to chase gold medals on your first attempt, it is often more efficient to enjoy the story first. You can always use the mission replay feature later to clean up any specific performance-based objectives. The final mission, "You Gotta Know When to Get Off," serves as the climactic end to the primary campaign and awards a prestigious gold trophy.

## Free Roam & Collectibles
Leonida is packed with hundreds of collectibles and free-roam activities that are vital for the 100% completion mark. From tagging WAiNK graffiti locations to finding hidden collectible cards, the world encourages meticulous exploration. You will also need to engage in diverse activities such as drone racing, fishing challenges, and mastering the shooting range. Stunt jumps remain a series staple, requiring you to launch vehicles off ramps with precise timing and speed. Many of these activities also reward you with unique gear or monetary bonuses that aid in other parts of your journey. Using a helicopter or a fast motorcycle can make the process of sweeping the map for these items much less tedious. Be sure to use the in-game map filters to stay organized and avoid missing small clusters of items in the more dense urban areas.

## Online/Multiplayer notes
While this guide focuses primarily on the single-player experience, the online component of Grand Theft Auto VI offers its own set of challenges. Engaging with the Leonida community allows you to participate in cooperative heists and competitive races that feel distinct from the main story. Many trophies in the base game can be earned solo, but having a reliable crew can make the more difficult challenges significantly easier. Ensure your internet connection is stable before attempting long-form multiplayer missions to avoid losing progress. Keep an eye out for limited-time events and seasonal updates that often introduce new trophies or rewards. Balancing your time between the solo narrative and the online world is key to experiencing everything the game has to offer. Remember to always play fair and respect the community guidelines to maintain a positive experience for everyone.

## Missable Trophies
Attention to detail is paramount if you want to avoid a second full playthrough due to missed opportunities. Several trophies are tied to specific story choices that can lock you out of certain outcomes if you aren't careful. For instance, the "Actions Speak Louder" trophy depends entirely on how you handle the game's pivotal decision points. Additionally, some partner-based stunts and robberies must be performed during specific windows of time when your companion is available. "Retail Therapy" and "Hot Together" are examples of trophies that require deliberate planning and execution before the story progresses too far. Always check your trophy list before starting a new chapter to ensure you haven't overlooked any time-sensitive tasks. If you do happen to miss one, don't panic; just ensure you have a backup save from earlier in the game.

## Platinum roadmap summary
The road to the Platinum, "Vice Got a Price!", is structured into six distinct stages for maximum efficiency. Begin by completing the story missions to unlock the full map and all primary game mechanics. Once the credits roll, transition into the stranger strands and side missions to further flesh out your completion percentage. The third stage involves tackling all the mini-games and skill-based challenges like the shooting range and races. Following this, dedicate time to the massive collectible hunt, sweeping the map for cards, tags, and meditation spots. The penultimate stage focuses on the economy, requiring you to spend large sums of cash and purchase various properties. Finally, use the platinum cleanup phase to mop up any remaining one-off stunts or missed trophies to secure your ultimate reward.

## Spoiler-free reading tips
To preserve the impact of the story's many twists, we recommend reading mission guides only when you find yourself genuinely stuck. The official trophy descriptions often hint at upcoming events, so browse the list with caution if you want a completely fresh experience. Focus on the "Getting Started" and "Free Roam" sections early on, as these contain general advice that won't ruin the plot. If a trophy description seems vague or reaction-based, like "What the hell just happened?", it is almost certainly a major story beat. Trust the roadmap structure provided in this guide to keep you on the right path without revealing the specific details of the narrative. Taking it slow and absorbing the atmosphere of Leonida is often the best way to enjoy the game anyway. Most importantly, remember that the journey is just as important as the destination in a world this rich.

## Trophy-by-Trophy Strategy: Early Game

One of the first trophies you should aim for is "Getting the Hang of It," which requires completing the initial driving lessons. Don't rush these; take your time to learn the handling characteristics of different vehicle classes as this will pay off for later stunt jumps.

Next, focus on "Early Bird Special" by successfully completing your first series of small-time robberies. The trick here is to scout the location thoroughly for alternate exits before triggering the heist, ensuring a quick and clean getaway from the local authorities.

For the "Tourist Trap" trophy, spend time exploring the immediate vicinity of the starting safehouse. Take photos of three major landmarks using your phone camera. This not only nets you a trophy but also populates your map with useful points of interest for later.

Don't ignore the "Street Smarts" trophy early on. This involves successfully outrunning a two-star wanted level in a stolen vehicle. Use alleys and tight turns to break line-of-sight quickly rather than trying to outrun them in a straight line on main roads.

When you unlock the first set of safehouse upgrades, aim for "Home Sweet Home." Focus your early cash earnings on buying functional upgrades rather than cosmetic items, as these will directly help with mission efficiency and survival.

Finally, tackle the "First Contact" trophy by completing all the initial introductory side quests given by the eccentric NPCs scattered around the starting area. These are usually short and provide a great way to earn a little extra cash early in your career.

## Trophy-by-Trophy Strategy: Mid Game

By mid-game, you should prioritize "Picture Perfect," which requires you to complete a specific set of photo challenges across Leonida. Use the in-game map to find these challenge locations, and make sure you are at the correct angle to capture the required elements.

The "Two's Company" trophy is a major mid-game goal, requiring you to successfully complete a complex, multi-stage heist with your companion. Coordinate your actions carefully, as failing to sync your movements can lead to instant mission failure.

"High-Speed Chase" challenges you to win five consecutive street races. Upgrade your engine and handling components at the local auto shop beforehand; don't try this with a stock vehicle, or you'll find yourself falling behind in the later stages of the race.

To earn "Drone Operator," you must master the drone flying mechanics. Spend some time in the practice mode before attempting the advanced time-trials, as the controls require a high degree of precision to navigate tight city spaces quickly.

"Big Spender" becomes achievable once your business ventures start to pay off. Invest in at least three different property types to unlock this. It’s worth holding off on some of the more expensive purchases until you have a more consistent income stream.

Lastly, "Co-op Conqueror" is a mid-game multiplayer trophy for completing a shared activity with another player. Whether it’s a race or a heist, the key is clear communication. Use the ping system to mark targets or objectives to stay coordinated.

## Advanced Tips

Mastering the weapon wheel is crucial for fast-paced combat. Practice switching between your primary sidearm and your heavy weapon without pausing, as this allows you to maintain momentum during intense firefights.

When tackling high-level missions, always check the weather conditions. Rainy nights offer better stealth opportunities for infiltration, while sunny days are perfect for high-speed chases and aerial reconnaissance.

Investing in passive income businesses early on is the secret to wealth in Leonida. Focus on businesses that require minimal oversight, allowing you to focus on the main story while your bank account grows.

For better control during stunt jumps, learn how to adjust your vehicle's pitch mid-air. This simple trick can be the difference between a successful landing and a catastrophic crash.

Don't neglect the side-missions involving street gangs. Completing these not only earns you cash but also improves your reputation in the criminal underworld, which can open up easier pathways in main missions.

Finally, always keep a spare set of clothes in your vehicle. Changing your outfit is an effective way to lose a wanted level, especially if you can quickly slip into an alleyway and swap your gear.

## FAQ

Q: Can I unlock all trophies in a single playthrough?
A: Most trophies are obtainable in a single run, but certain story choices can lock you out of specific side content. Plan carefully if you want to avoid a second playthrough.

Q: Are there any trophies that are entirely multiplayer-focused?
A: Yes, a small subset of the trophies requires interaction with the online mode, but the vast majority can be earned through the single-player campaign.

Q: How do I track my progress for the 100% completion trophy?
A: Use the in-game statistics menu, which breaks down your progress by activity, missions completed, and collectibles gathered.

Q: If I miss a stunt jump, can I go back and do it later?
A: Absolutely. Almost all collectibles and stunt jumps remain accessible in the open world long after their corresponding story missions have been completed.

Q: Do gold medal mission requirements affect trophy progress?
A: Only for specific "all-star" type trophies. Most story trophies just require you to complete the mission, regardless of your performance rating.

Q: What’s the fastest way to travel across the map?
A: Using helicopters or high-performance motorcycles is generally faster than cars, especially when you need to avoid heavy traffic congestion in the city center.

Q: Does difficulty level affect trophy unlocking?
A: No, there are no difficulty-specific trophies in GTA VI. You can play on any difficulty setting and still unlock all associated trophies.

Q: What should I do if a trophy fails to unlock?
A: First, check your console's achievement tracker. If it didn't register, try restarting the game or re-loading a save file from just before the trigger condition was met.

Q: Are there any time-limited collectibles?
A: Some collectibles only appear during specific in-game events or story phases. Check the map regularly for temporary markers.

Q: Can I change my character's appearance?
A: Yes, visit any barber shop or clothing store to customize your character's look. Some trophies are linked to specific clothing items.

Q: Is there an easy way to escape from the police?
A: Use high-speed vehicles and avoid straight roads. Ducking into low-traffic areas or changing your vehicle is highly effective.

Q: Are there any missable side missions?
A: Yes, some side missions are only available during certain chapters of the story. Keep an eye on your map for side quest markers.

Q: Can I store multiple vehicles?
A: Yes, you can purchase garages to store your favorite cars and motorcycles throughout the city.

Q: How do I earn money quickly?
A: Engaging in high-reward side activities like street races or stock market manipulation is often more efficient than grinding small-time robberies.

Q: Do I need to complete every single side quest for 100% completion?
A: Most, but not all. The 100% completion tracker will indicate which activities contribute to the total percentage.
