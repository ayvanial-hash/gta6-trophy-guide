# GTA VI Trophy Hunting Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to the ultimate guide for conquering the sun-drenched streets of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI. Achieving the Platinum trophy in this sprawling masterpiece requires a blend of criminal ambition, tactical precision, and a lot of patience. This manual is designed to steer you through the complexities of the game's reward system without stripping away the joy of discovery. Whether you are a series veteran or a newcomer to the Vice City lifestyle, these pages contain everything you need to know. We will cover every aspect from the main narrative arc to the most obscure side activities. Prepare yourself for a long but rewarding journey toward becoming a true legend of the underworld.

## Getting Started
Before you dive headfirst into the chaos, it is essential to understand the basic mechanics that drive progression. Start by familiarizing yourself with the new partner system, as many trophies are tied to your synergy with your companion. It is highly recommended to keep multiple save files, especially before embarking on major heist sequences or making pivotal story choices. The game world is massive, so investing in fast vehicles early on will save you significant travel time between missions. Don't feel pressured to complete every side activity immediately; most can be tackled at your own pace during the endgame. Keep a steady eye on your in-game statistics menu to track your progress toward 100% completion. Lastly, always carry enough ammunition and body armor to handle the unpredictable escalations of Leonida’s law enforcement.

## Story Trophies
The narrative backbone of GTA VI is divided into several chapters, each offering its own set of unique trophies. Most of these are unmissable and will unlock naturally as you progress through the main missions of Jason and Lucia’s journey. Focus on finishing the "Gone Fishing" prologue to establish your footing in the world before moving into the heavier heists. Missions like "The Cartel Heist" and "Breakfast Run" are key milestones that signify your rising influence in the criminal hierarchy. While it might be tempting to chase gold medals on your first attempt, it is often more efficient to enjoy the story first. You can always use the mission replay feature later to clean up any specific performance-based objectives. The final mission, "You Gotta Know When to Get Off," serves as the climactic end to the primary campaign and awards a prestigious gold trophy.

## Free Roam & Collectibles
Leonida is packed with hundreds of collectibles and free-roam activities that are vital for the 100% completion mark. From tagging WAiNK graffiti locations to finding hidden collectible cards, the world encourages meticulous exploration. You will also need to engage in diverse activities such as drone racing, fishing challenges, and mastering the shooting range. Stunt jumps remain a series staple, requiring you to launch vehicles off ramps with precise timing and speed. Many of these activities also reward you with unique gear or monetary bonuses that aid in other parts of your journey. Using a helicopter or a fast motorcycle can make the process of sweeping the map for these items much less tedious. Be sure to use the in-game map filters to stay organized and avoid missing small clusters of items in the more dense urban areas.

## Online/Multiplayer notes
While this guide focuses primarily on the single-player experience, the online component of Grand Theft Auto VI offers its own set of challenges. Engaging with the Leonida community allows you to participate in cooperative heists and competitive races that feel distinct from the main story. Many trophies in the base game can be earned solo, but having a reliable crew can make the more difficult challenges significantly easier. Ensure your internet connection is stable before attempting long-form multiplayer missions to avoid losing progress. Keep an eye out for limited-time events and seasonal updates that often introduce new trophies or rewards. Balancing your time between the solo narrative and the online world is key to experiencing everything the game has to offer. Remember to always play fair and respect the community guidelines to maintain a positive experience for everyone.

## Missable Trophies
Attention to detail is paramount if you want to avoid a second full playthrough due to missed opportunities. Several trophies are tied to specific story choices that can lock you out of certain outcomes if you aren't careful. For instance, the "Actions Speak Louder" trophy depends entirely on how you handle the game's pivotal decision points. Additionally, some partner-based stunts and robberies must be performed during specific windows of time when your companion is available. "Retail Therapy" and "Hot Together" are examples of trophies that require deliberate planning and execution before the story progresses too far. Always check your trophy list before starting a new chapter to ensure you haven't overlooked any time-sensitive tasks. If you do happen to miss one, don't panic; just ensure you have a backup save from earlier in the game.

## Platinum roadmap summary
The road to the Platinum, "Vice Got a Price!", is structured into six distinct stages for maximum efficiency. Begin by completing the story missions to unlock the full map and all primary game mechanics. Once the credits roll, transition into the stranger strands and side missions to further flesh out your completion percentage. The third stage involves tackling all the mini-games and skill-based challenges like the shooting range and races. Following this, dedicate time to the massive collectible hunt, sweeping the map for cards, tags, and meditation spots. The penultimate stage focuses on the economy, requiring you to spend large sums of cash and purchase various properties. Finally, use the platinum cleanup phase to mop up any remaining one-off stunts or missed trophies to secure your ultimate reward.

## Spoiler-free reading tips
To preserve the impact of the story's many twists, we recommend reading mission guides only when you find yourself genuinely stuck. The official trophy descriptions often hint at upcoming events, so browse the list with caution if you want a completely fresh experience. Focus on the "Getting Started" and "Free Roam" sections early on, as these contain general advice that won't ruin the plot. If a trophy description seems vague or reaction-based, like "What the hell just happened?", it is almost certainly a major story beat. Trust the roadmap structure provided in this guide to keep you on the right path without revealing the specific details of the narrative. Taking it slow and absorbing the atmosphere of Leonida is often the best way to enjoy the game anyway. Most importantly, remember that the journey is just as important as the destination in a world this rich.
