About this guide

An offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI, built from the official trophy list as PlayStation publishes it (names, descriptions and grades as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree).

Every trophy's guide cites its sources beside it, in the detail pane and as a structured sources list in data/trophies.json — named guides such as PowerPyx, PSNProfiles and Exophase. The official names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them, including the asterisked name and every curly quote, dash and ellipsis. The guides are written in this entry's own words: they draw on the official descriptions and named guides, and note where a condition is inferred from the description's wording.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back; import merges with what you have and only accepts names this list holds. The app loads nothing from the network: every file it uses is its own, and links to sources open only when you follow them.

Tip: press / to jump to search, 1–4 to switch tabs.
