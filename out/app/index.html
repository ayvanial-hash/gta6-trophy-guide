About this guide

An offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI, built from the official trophy list as PlayStation publishes it (names, descriptions and grades as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree).

Every trophy's guide cites its sources beside it. The official names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them, including the asterisked name and every curly quote, dash and ellipsis. The guides are written in this entry's own words: they draw on the official descriptions and note where a condition is inferred from the description's wording.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back. The app loads nothing from the network: every file it uses is its own.

Select a trophy to read its guide.
