# Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Trophy Hunting Guide

This document contains essential tips and strategies to help you achieve 100% completion in Grand Theft Auto VI.

## Quick Wins First
The easiest trophies to secure early are exploration-based, specifically those tied to landmark discoveries. Prioritize unlocking the map by visiting all major tourist hubs within the first few hours of gameplay. This not only ticks off a trophy but also makes fast travel options much more accessible later.

Don't overlook the simple social interaction trophies. Greet NPCs and engage in minor activities like store robberies or street races early on. These are low-stakes opportunities to build up your character's reputation metrics, which often serve as prerequisites for more complex, high-tier progression trophies.

Check the activity log frequently in the first few hours. Many seemingly random encounters are linked to specific hidden trophies that are easy to miss if you rush the main story missions. By completing these minor side activities early, you clear the clutter from your quest log.

Finally, spend time customizing your character's starting appearance immediately. Many early-game achievements trigger upon first modifying your look or purchasing your first set of clothing. These are virtually free points that help boost your early trophy total without requiring significant mechanical mastery.

## Settings That Save Time
Configure your control scheme to prioritize accessibility settings for aim assist and traversal. Enabling high-level aim assist can significantly reduce the difficulty of combat-focused trophies, allowing you to breeze through waves of enemies without frustration.

Adjust your map filtering settings to display only essential items like collectibles or specific shop icons. This clutter reduction helps you focus on your current trophy-hunting goal and prevents the map from becoming an overwhelming sea of icons that distracts you from your target.

Enable automatic saving prompts and adjust the frequency of autosaves to ensure you never lose progress before attempting a high-risk trophy. Having a recent save to reload is crucial if you fail a timed challenge or miss a critical interaction.

Turn on subtitle and UI notifications for all events. Being alerted visually to environmental changes or triggered events can save minutes of searching for a location or objective. It’s a small setting tweak that pays huge dividends over long play sessions.

## Combat Trophy Tactics
Master the cover system immediately, as many combat trophies require surviving encounters with high enemy density. Never stay out in the open; always identify your next piece of cover before making your next move in a firefight.

Focus on upgrading your weaponry early. Many combat trophies are far easier when you have access to specialized ammunition or upgraded firearm mods. Invest your initial money into weapon attachments that improve accuracy and damage output rather than purely aesthetic upgrades.

Utilize the environment in every combat scenario. From explosive canisters to breakable scenery, using the surroundings as an extension of your arsenal can clear out enemies faster than standard gunplay. This is particularly effective for those "multikill" or "environmental takedown" trophies.

Don't be afraid to retreat and reset if an engagement turns sour. Trophy progress for combat often relies on meeting specific criteria like "don't take damage" or "complete within X seconds." Tactical repositioning is often safer than brute-forcing a difficult combat encounter.

## Driving & Traversal Trophies
Always keep a vehicle in good condition. Many driving trophies are impossible if you're piloting a wrecked car that handles unpredictably. Regularly visiting a repair shop is a necessary time investment to maintain the performance required for tight time trials.

Map out your routes before starting a long-distance traversal challenge. Familiarity with shortcuts, off-road paths, and hidden alleyways is the key to shaving seconds off your best times for races and courier missions. Memorize the city layout early.

Use the game’s drift mechanics to handle sharp corners at high speeds. Learning to drift effectively allows you to maintain momentum through city streets without needing to tap the brakes constantly, which is vital for winning timed events.

Upgrade your vehicle's engine and suspension as soon as possible. Better handling and higher top speed allow you to overcome minor driving errors that would otherwise result in failing a time-based trophy. Speed is just as important as skill in these challenges.

## Money Farming For Purchase Trophies
Focus on high-reward missions that offer consistent payouts. Avoid spending time on low-value activities like petty theft; instead, target specific high-value assets or side missions that offer substantial rewards for completion.

Invest your early profits wisely. Buying businesses or assets that generate passive income is the most effective way to reach the high-money totals required for certain trophies. Let your money work for you rather than grinding manual tasks.

Watch the stock market. Certain in-game events can trigger massive fluctuations in stock prices. Timing your investments to coincide with these events is the fastest way to turn a small bankroll into the fortune needed for end-game purchases.

Don't neglect the side-quest rewards. Many side missions provide substantial cash bonuses that are overlooked by players solely focused on the main storyline. Treat these quests as dedicated money-farming opportunities to reach your goal faster.

## Photo Mode & Hidden Trophies
Master the photo mode tools early. Many hidden trophies require photographing specific, rare, or hard-to-reach locations. Spend time exploring the map’s high-altitude vantage points to scout potential photo opportunities.

Pay attention to environmental cues for hidden trophies. Often, unusual graffiti, specific NPCs, or out-of-place objects indicate a hidden collectible or trophy trigger. If it looks out of place, investigate it closely, as it’s likely part of an achievement.

Experiment with the photo mode’s depth of field and filters. Some trophies require a specific "look" for the photo to count. If a photo doesn't trigger a trophy, adjust your camera settings or angle slightly before trying again.

## Time-Saver Checklist
*   Unlock all fast travel points within the first hour.
*   Prioritize upgrading your inventory capacity to reduce shop visits.
*   Always carry a variety of weapons for different combat scenarios.
*   Set up a recurring save file for every major trophy attempt.
*   Use the map filter to highlight only mission-critical objectives.
*   Complete all low-level side quests before starting long mission chains.
*   Always repair your car before starting a new time trial.
*   Invest in passive income businesses as soon as you have the funds.
*   Listen for ambient audio cues that hint at nearby secrets.
*   Use high-ground vantage points to scout missions before entering.
*   Keep a notepad or document tracking your trophy progress.
*   Use the photo mode to scout enemies in heavily fortified areas.
*   Disable unnecessary HUD elements to see the environment better.
*   Focus on one trophy category at a time to stay focused.
*   Always check the reward before starting a new side mission.

## Common Time Wasters To Avoid
Avoid getting distracted by random NPC interactions that don't lead to mission objectives. While fun, they rarely contribute to your trophy goals and can consume massive amounts of time that could be better spent on productive activities.

Do not grind for money by performing repetitive, low-value tasks once better opportunities have unlocked. Always transition to the most profitable activity available as soon as your character stats allow it to avoid stagnating.

Stop trying to brute-force difficult combat trophies without the right gear. It is often faster to spend time farming the money to upgrade your weapons than to repeatedly die in a difficult fight while under-equipped.

Avoid aimlessly driving around the city when you need to reach a destination. Use the map, identify the fastest route, and stick to it. Every minute spent wandering is a minute taken away from your progress towards your ultimate trophy completion.

## Final Thoughts
Follow these guidelines diligently, and you will be well on your way to earning every trophy this game has to offer. Good luck, and enjoy your time in the city.
